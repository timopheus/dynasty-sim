Twelve middle-aged men of the Kingdom of Aurenne, early 18th century France: powdered or long curled wigs, some with their own tied-back hair, lace jabots or cravats, embroidered coats in red, blue and green with gold buttons, a few officers with gold braid; fair to olive skin.
Every face is different: thin and heavy faces, long and round faces, narrow and broad noses, some bald, some with scars or moles, some with moustaches, varied eye colours; no two people look alike.
Layout: a character sheet grid of twelve different people, four across and three down, twelve equal passport photos that touch edge to edge, each photo the same size; every photo shows a different person with a different face, hair and build.
Pose in every photo: an official passport photograph, strictly front-facing and symmetrical, head straight, looking straight into the camera, neutral expression, mouth closed; head and shoulders with the upper chest; a clear empty margin above the head and on both sides; the head never touches the edge of its photo.
Style: simple flat pixel art in the style of the passport photos in the game Papers, Please: a clean dark outline around every head and figure, flat colour areas with at most one darker shade, few details, no dithering, no texture, no noise, no stray pixels. Plain flat grey-pink background in every photo.
Details that may appear on some people: earrings, a necklace, a brooch, a scar across a cheek or brow, blue, green or brown eyes, a mole, a moustache or beard on some men. Middle-aged people, 30 to 50 years old.
