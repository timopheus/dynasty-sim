One-colour black line art portrait in the style of the passport photos in Papers, Please: a man about 30 years old, early 18th century Europe. Only pure black lines and solid black shapes on an empty white background: one continuous unbroken black outline around the whole head, ears, neck and shoulders; black hair as a solid black shape; eyes, brows, nose, mouth and a few facial lines drawn as thin black lines; plain dark coat as a solid black shape with a light collar left white. No grey, no skin colour, no shading, no half-tones, no dithering, no patterns, no texture. Strictly front-facing passport photo: head and body facing the viewer squarely, shoulders level, looking straight at the camera, neutral expression, head and shoulders, the head in the upper middle of the frame.
