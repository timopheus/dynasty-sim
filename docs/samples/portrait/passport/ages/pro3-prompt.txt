Minimalist 1-bit pixel art character age sheet in the exact style of the passport photos in the game Papers, Please, like the reference sheet. Black ink only on a plain white background, like figures cut out of black paper and drawn with a thick black marker.
Layout: a grid of six equal tall passport photos, three across and two down, the photos touch each other with no gaps, divided only by thin one-pixel black lines, and fill the whole picture edge to edge. Every photo shows the same man, early 18th century Europe, at a different age: top row from left to right at 8, 13 and 17 years old; bottom row from left to right at 35, 50 and 65 years old. The same face shape, the same nose, the same brows and the same hair in every photo; only the age changes. The child has realistic proportions and small eyes drawn the same way as the adult's, not cartoon eyes.
Framing in every photo: the figure is drawn smaller inside its photo, a half-length view: the head, the shoulders, the chest and the upper body down to the waist are visible, the body reaches the bottom of the photo. A wide empty white space above the head and on both sides of the head; the head never touches the frame.
Pose in every photo: an official passport photograph, strictly front-facing and symmetrical, head straight, looking straight into the camera, neutral expression, mouth closed.
Outline: in every photo one thick, continuous, closed black outline runs all around the head, the ears, the jaw, the chin, the neck and the shoulders with no gaps; the inside of each face stays white.
Hair: one solid black shape with a clean edge.
Face: a few clean black strokes only: two eyes with a black pupil each, two short brows, a nose drawn as a simple line along one side, a mouth as one clear horizontal line; wrinkles as single clean lines only for the older ages.
Clothes: the same early 18th century clothes in all six photos, the child included: a long buttoned coat as one solid black shape with a white neckcloth tied at the throat; no modern shirt collars.
Strictly forbidden: grey, shading, shadows, half-tones, dithering, hatching, texture, patterns, stray single pixels, dots or noise, a background colour, text, numbers.
