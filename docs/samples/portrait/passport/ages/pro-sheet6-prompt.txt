Minimalist 1-bit pixel art character age sheet in the exact style of the passport photos in the game Papers, Please. Black ink only on a plain white background, like figures cut out of black paper and drawn with a thick black marker.
Layout: a grid of six equal passport photos, three across and two down, separated by clear white gaps. Every photo shows the same man, early 18th century Europe, at a different age: top row from left to right at 8, 13 and 17 years old; bottom row from left to right at 35, 50 and 65 years old. The same face, the same hair colour and the same features in every photo; only the age changes: the child has a small round face, large eyes and a small nose; the youth is slim; the adult is in his prime; the old man has thinner receding hair and a few clean lines on the forehead and around the mouth.
Pose in every photo: an official passport photograph, strictly front-facing and symmetrical, head straight, looking straight into the camera, neutral expression, mouth closed; the head in the upper part of its photo with an empty white margin above the hair and on both sides; the chest and the coat fill the lower part down to the bottom of the photo.
Outline: one thick, continuous, closed black outline, two pixels wide, runs all around each head, the ears, the jaw, the chin, the neck and the shoulders with no gaps anywhere; the inside of each face stays white.
Hair: one solid black shape with a clean edge, no strands.
Face: drawn with a few clean black strokes only: two clear eyes with a black pupil each, two short straight brows, a nose drawn as a simple line along one side and a small line for the nostrils, a mouth drawn as one clear horizontal line.
Clothes: a plain coat as one solid black shape with a white neckcloth outlined in black.
Strictly forbidden: grey, shading, shadows, half-tones, dithering, hatching, texture, patterns, stray single pixels, dots or noise, a background colour, frames, borders, text, numbers.
